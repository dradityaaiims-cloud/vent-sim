# Ventilator knowledge quiz: Form A

(CC BY 4.0.) 10 single-best-answer questions, about 10 minutes. Do not use notes or the simulator. Answers are in `quiz-answer-key.md`. Use the same participant code on every form.

**Participant code (not your name):** ________

**1.** A 52-year-old woman, 160 cm tall and 80 kg, has ARDS. Which tidal volume equals 6 mL/kg of predicted body weight?
A. 315 mL
B. 480 mL
C. 640 mL
D. 800 mL

**2.** A patient on volume control has a sudden rise in peak pressure from 24 to 40 cmH2O. An inspiratory hold shows a plateau of 19 cmH2O, unchanged from earlier. What is the most likely cause?
A. Mucus plug or biting the endotracheal tube
B. Tension pneumothorax
C. Right mainstem intubation
D. Worsening pulmonary edema

**3.** Tidal volume 420 mL, plateau pressure 26 cmH2O, total PEEP 8 cmH2O. What are the driving pressure and static compliance?
A. 18 cmH2O; 16 mL/cmH2O
B. 26 cmH2O; 16 mL/cmH2O
C. 8 cmH2O; 53 mL/cmH2O
D. 18 cmH2O; 23 mL/cmH2O

**4.** Which finding on the flow-time waveform indicates auto-PEEP?
A. High peak inspiratory flow
B. Expiratory flow has not returned to zero when the next breath starts
C. A square inspiratory flow pattern
D. Brief, rapid expiratory flow

**5.** A 70-year-old with severe COPD becomes hypotensive (MAP 52) after intubation and manual bagging at about 30 breaths/min. Airway pressures are high and there is wheeze. What is the best immediate action?
A. Increase the set rate to blow off CO2
B. Give a fluid bolus only and keep the same settings
C. Disconnect briefly to allow exhalation, then lower the rate, shorten inspiratory time and treat bronchospasm
D. Increase PEEP to 15 cmH2O

**6.** In ARDS on FiO2 0.8, PEEP 8, tidal volume 6 mL/kg PBW, plateau 24, driving pressure 11, SpO2 is 86% with a normal blood pressure. What is a reasonable next step?
A. Lower PEEP to reduce barotrauma
B. Raise tidal volume to 10 mL/kg
C. Raise PEEP stepwise while watching plateau, driving pressure and blood pressure
D. Reduce FiO2

**7.** A young woman with diabetic ketoacidosis is intubated. Before intubation her rate was 30 and PaCO2 22. The ventilator is set to rate 12, VT 450; now pH 7.02, PaCO2 52. What is the best approach?
A. Lower the rate further
B. Give sodium bicarbonate only
C. Raise PEEP
D. Match her previous minute ventilation with a higher rate and recheck the ABG

**8.** On a spontaneous breathing trial the respiratory rate is 22 and the exhaled tidal volume is 0.44 L. What is the RSBI and what does it suggest?
A. 50, predicts failure
B. 500, predicts failure
C. 50, favors success
D. 5, favors success

**9.** On volume control with a fixed flow, the airway pressure waveform is scooped (concave) during inspiration and the patient looks distressed. What does this indicate?
A. Delivered flow is lower than the patient's demand
B. Pneumothorax
C. A circuit leak
D. Auto-PEEP

**10.** A patient with COPD on pressure support shows a pressure spike at the end of inspiration and a slowly decaying flow; the patient is trying to exhale against the breath. Which adjustment helps most?
A. Lower the cycle-off percentage
B. Raise the cycle-off percentage
C. Increase the rise time
D. Raise PEEP
